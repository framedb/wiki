- AO Stadium is an [[acetate]] [[Ophthalmic]] frame
- It was produced from at least after 1963 up until [[1970s]]
	- This is because [AO catalog from 1963](((6835dbfc-f460-4729-a263-6f2ed0ee82ab))) did not include the frame
	- Though this might have been omission - not all of AO products had to appear in the catalog
